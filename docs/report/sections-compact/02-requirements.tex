\req{FR-10} & Retain the source span every token came from. \\
\req{FR-11} & Classify each word into one of the defined terminal classes. \\
\req{FR-12} & Carry a canonical form, donor-language label and gloss on every token. \\

\req{FR-15} & Tolerate missing or added diacritics without losing attested homograph distinctions. \\

\req{FR-18} & Author the grammar as a CFG in natural form, left recursion included. \\
\req{FR-19} & Eliminate left recursion mechanically. \\
\req{FR-20} & Apply left factoring until no common prefixes remain. \\
\req{FR-21} & Record every transformation with the rules it consumed and produced. \\
\req{FR-22} & Compute FIRST and FOLLOW sets and construct an LL(1) parsing table. \\
\req{FR-23} & Detect and report table conflicts rather than resolving them silently. \\
\req{FR-24} & Read the token stream and return an explicit accept or reject verdict. \\
\req{FR-25} & Return the derivation as an ordered list of production identifiers on acceptance. \\
\req{FR-26} & Report the error position and the legal terminal set on rejection. \\
\req{FR-27} & Offer deterministic repair suggestions for rejected input. \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l@{}}{\textit{Presentation and access}}\\
\req{FR-28} & Let visitors analyse a statement without authenticating; do not store such input. \\
\req{FR-29} & Display the original grammar, ledger, transformed grammar, FIRST/FOLLOW and LL(1) table. \\
\req{FR-30} & Search the lexicon incrementally, tolerant of missing accents. \\
\req{FR-31} & Require authentication for corpus access and evidence export. \\
\req{FR-32} & Detect conversation topics and cite the words that triggered each. \\

\subsection{Non-functional requirements}

\begin{center}
\scriptsize
\begin{tabular}{@{}l l p{9.6cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Id} & \textbf{Quality} & \textbf{Requirement} \\
\midrule
\req{NFR-1} & Determinism     & Identical input under identical grammar and lexicon versions always produces an identical result. No randomness, no model inference. \\
\req{NFR-2} & Traceability    & Every reported claim cites its evidence: a rule id, statement id or source span. \\
\req{NFR-3} & Correctness     & The parsing table is conflict-free, enforced by an automated test rather than asserted. \\
\req{NFR-4} & Integrity       & Domain objects are immutable and reject unknown fields at construction. \\
\req{NFR-5} & Security        & Session cookies are \texttt{HttpOnly} and \texttt{Secure}; state-changing requests require a CSRF token and a same-origin check. \\
\req{NFR-6} & Accessibility   & The interface meets WCAG 2.1 AA, verified by automated scans on every view. \\
\req{NFR-7} & Portability     & The service runs as a single container with no external database server. \\
\req{NFR-8} & Reproducibility & Report figures and tables are generated from the running system, never transcribed. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subsection{Constraints and acceptance criteria}

The analyzer accepts Cameroonian Francanglais only; the target is fixed in code and
requests naming another variety are refused. The scanner, lexer and parser are written by
hand --- using \texttt{lex}/\texttt{yacc} would hide precisely the transformations this
coursework assesses. The parser is LL(1) by construction, so any rule that cannot be
expressed within one token of lookahead must be reported as a limitation rather than
worked around. Speakers are informed and consent obtained before identifiable
conversation is retained.

The system meets its requirements when: the corpus holds at least ten manually attested
field statements, each attributable to a collector; the parsing table contains no
conflicting cell; every collected statement receives an explicit verdict with a
derivation or an expected-terminal set; and the automated suite passes.
Section~\ref{c:verification} reports the measured outcome.
